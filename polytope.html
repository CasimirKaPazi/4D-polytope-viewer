8 Cubic Cells

Click a cell to show it alone (Single Cell mode).

Polytope selection: The six convex regular 4-polytopes (5-, 8-, 16-, 24-, 600-, 120-cell), the uniform 48-cell and snub 24-cell, the disphenoidal 288-cell, the A4 and B4 root polytopes, the omnitruncated 5-cell, and the E8 roots projected to 4D.

Omnitruncated 5-cell: The permutohedron of order 5. Each vertex is a ranking of five candidates A–E, placed at its Borda score vector; edges swap two candidates adjacent in the ranking, so path length is the Kendall tau distance. Each cell holds the rankings that put one group of candidates above all the others (labels like "AB ≻ CDE"). The averaged Borda vector of any set of ballots lies inside the polytope.

How to view: Cross your eyes until the two images merge into one central 3D image.

Decompositions: Ways the active polytope splits into smaller polytopes. 24-cell: triality (3 disjoint 16-cells), 16-cell + tesseract, and 3 overlapping tesseracts. Tesseract: 2 inscribed 16-cells (alternating vertex parity). 16-cell: 2 inscribed tetrahedra. 288-cell: its two 24-cells. 600-cell: 24-cell + snub 24-cell, 5 disjoint 24-cells, or all 25 inscribed 24-cells. 120-cell: 5 disjoint 600-cells or 120 disjoint 5-cells. Omnitruncated 5-cell: by winner or by loser (5 truncated octahedra each). The polytope's own edges go gray; the decomposition's groups appear in distinct hues. When groups overlap, vertex colors blend - the 3-tesseract case gives the three secondary colors (yellow, cyan, magenta), each marking a specific pair of tesseracts.

Cell highlighting: Visualizes the 3D cells forming the boundary (5-, 8-, 16-, 24-cell and omnitruncated 5-cell). Click a cell in the legend to show it alone.

3D Projection: Projects the 4D polytope onto a 3D hyperplane (W axis, or (1,1,0,0) for the 24-cell), then rotates the result in 3D. Color shows the height along the projection axis. For the 24-cell this shows the decomposition 24 = 2 + 6 + 8 + 8 relative to the chosen axis.

Axes view: Draws the four 4D coordinate axes through the origin (red X, yellow-green Y, cyan Z, purple W).

Lattice tiling: Tesseract, 16-cell, and 24-cell tile 4D space. Shows the center plus every neighbor sharing a cell with it (8 for the tesseract, 16 for the 16-cell, 24 for the 24-cell), each obtained by reflecting the center polytope through that shared cell.

Hexachromatic: Stereoscopic 2D color space. Left eye colors by angle in XZ, right eye by angle in YW. The brain fuses them into a unique color per S³ point.

3-Sphere view: Stereographic projection from S³ to R³. Geodesics become circular arcs.

Fullscreen: F, the Fullscreen button, or a double-click on the picture shows only the two images, filling the screen; mouse and keys keep working, and the cursor hides when idle. F, Esc or a double-click leaves it.

Mouse / touch: Drag in left window for XY/XZ (shift: XZ/XW), in right window for YZ/YW (shift: YW/ZW). Rotations act in the fixed viewing frame. Wheel to zoom.

Keyboard: Space pause/resume, R reset rotation, F fullscreen, ← → previous/next polytope, D next decomposition, C cell highlighting, P 3D projection, A axes, H hexachromatic, G gradients, L lattice, T trails, M speed mutation, O orthographic, S 3-sphere view. Shortcuts are also shown on the buttons.
